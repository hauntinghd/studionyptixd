Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

NYPTID Studio ("Studio," "we," "us") is operated by NYPTID Industries. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

YouTube data (if you connect a channel)

Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Scopes we may request:

  • youtube.readonly — channel and video metadata
  • yt-analytics.readonly — analytics (CTR, retention, impressions)
  • youtube.force-ssl — secure API operations
  • youtube.upload — only when you explicitly publish a video to your channel

We do not sell YouTube data. You can disconnect anytime in Settings → YouTube or via Google Account permissions.

Other data we collect

  • Account email and plan status (Supabase auth, Stripe/PayPal)
  • Content you create in Studio (scripts, renders, prompts)
  • Technical logs (IP, user-agent) for security and operations

Sub-processors

Supabase, RunPod, Fal.ai, PayPal, Stripe, Vercel, and Google (when you connect YouTube).
