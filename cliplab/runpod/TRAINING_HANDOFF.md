# ClipLab offline training archive

RunPod production execution and all RunPod operator tooling are permanently
retired. Studio and ClipLab production are Contabo-owned.

The model definitions, dataset bootstrap utilities, and training programs in
this directory remain only for offline research. Training does not activate a
runtime backend, publish an image, transfer weights to an endpoint, or alter
Studio provider policy. The operator/deployment entrypoints are credential-free
fail-fast tombstones and must stay that way.
