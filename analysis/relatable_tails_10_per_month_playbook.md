# RelatableTails — 10 Shorts/Month Compounding Playbook

**Goal:** ~10 posts/month, evergreen views, ad revenue — not daily grind.

**Model:** [MoMike — dead channel revival via decision-maker Shorts](https://youtu.be/CVdZ9d3M57Y)

---

## Why 10/month can work (if format is right)

YouTube Shorts **library compounding** — each video keeps getting Browse/Suggested impressions for months if:
1. **Hook is universal** (pet owners already ask this question)
2. **Format is identical** (algorithm learns your channel bucket)
3. **Comments = next video** (engagement loop without new ideas)
4. **Controversy lite** (comments, not outrage)

MoMike: fortune-telling decision maker, question → reveal → comment firestorm.  
RelatableTails: **pet decision maker** — same engine, pet skin.

---

## The one template (film 10 in one afternoon)

| Sec | Beat |
|-----|------|
| 0–1 | Text hook: question people already Google |
| 1–3 | Pet clip / reaction (or locked 3D tail character later) |
| 3–5 | "Decision maker" card: YES / NO / IT'S COMPLICATED |
| 5–8 | One controversial line VO (8–12 words max) |
| 8–10 | CTA: "Comment your pet's name — we'll decide next" |

**Rules:**
- Same visual layout every time (brand = format)
- VO under 15 seconds
- No day-specific trends — evergreen questions only

---

## Month 1 — 10 shorts (batch queue)

Post ~2–3/week (Tue / Thu / Sat). Same title pattern: `Does your [pet] actually [X]?`

1. Does your cat love you — or just the feeder?
2. Your dog chose them over you (here's why)
3. Is your cat ignoring you on purpose?
4. Does your pet know when you're sad?
5. Why your dog stares at you while you eat
6. Cat 3AM zoomies: punishment or love?
7. Golden retriever energy vs your actual dog
8. Does your pet understand when you're leaving?
9. Boy dogs vs girl dogs — who’s more loyal? (comment bait)
10. What your pet's sleep spot says about you

---

## Virality without volume

| Lever | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Comment mining** | Pin: "Drop a question — top comment becomes Short #11 next month" |
| **Controversy dial** | One mildly wrong take per video (MoMike: "boy best friends don't exist") |
| **Series branding** | End card: "RelatableTails Decision #7" — bingeable |
| **Cross-post long-form** | None required — Shorts feed is self-contained |
| **Republish winner** | After 30 days, new thumb + `#2` on same question if it hit 50k+ |

---

## Money math (conservative)

- 10 Shorts × 100k views avg (one breakout pulls the month) ≈ 500k–1M/mo over time as library grows
- Shorts RPM ~$0.05–0.15 → **$25–150/mo** early, compounds as backlog hits 30–60 videos
- Real target month 3+: **30 shorts in library**, 10 new/month → prior 20 still earning

---

## Production batch (once/month, ~3 hours)

1. **30 min** — pick 10 questions from comments + Reddit r/cats r/dogs
2. **60 min** — write 10 × 40-word scripts
3. **90 min** — generate clips (pet stock + text overlay + decision card)
4. **30 min** — schedule in YouTube Studio

No daily posting. No trend chasing. **One format, comment-driven sequel queue.**
